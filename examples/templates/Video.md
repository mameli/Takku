Write notes on a video for someone who will not watch it: a talk, a tutorial, an interview or a podcast found online. This is a video, not a meeting: one or more people speak to an audience, nobody decides anything or takes on tasks, so there are no decisions, actions or next steps to look for.

### In short
Three to five bullets: what the video is about and the main points or conclusions, each a full sentence that stands on its own. If the speakers argue for something, say what and why.

Then the topics, in the order the video covers them:

- For each topic a `###` heading with a short, concrete title, followed by the time it starts in the Transcript, as `### Title (12:30)`.
- Under each topic a bullet list: the claims, the reasoning behind them, the examples, figures and comparisons given, with sub-bullets for details. Keep concrete examples and numbers: they are what the video adds.
- A step-by-step explanation (a tutorial, a setup, a recipe) stays a numbered list, in order, with commands, settings and values exactly as said.
- In an interview or a podcast, say who holds a view only when the speakers say their names; otherwise write the point without a subject. Never write "Speaker 1".
- A video often has one host who talks about their own posts, products and channel, sometimes by name: that is the host, not someone else. Do not turn the host into a third person the video "reacts to".
- Opinions stay opinions: "X thinks that…" or "according to the speaker…", not stated as facts.
- Recognition gets names, products and technical terms wrong: write the correct one when the subject makes it certain; when it is not certain, leave the passage out.
- Leave out intros, requests to like and subscribe, channel news and passages that make no sense.
- Leave out sponsor segments entirely: a product presented because it pays for the video ("today's sponsor", "thanks to X for sponsoring", a discount code, a link in the description), however long the segment and however related to the topic. Never name the sponsor anywhere in the notes, not even to say it was left out, and no heading mentions it.

### Mentioned
Always write this section when the video names any book, paper, tool, product, library, company, person or link, which is almost always: one bullet each, `**Name**: why it came up`, in the order they first appear. Products the topics above already discuss go here too. Leave out this section, heading included, only if the video names none.

Write every heading, these ones included, in the language of the summary.
