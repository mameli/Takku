Write the minutes of a Governance Meeting of a circle, run according to Holacracy with Integrative Decision Making. The circle changes its structure (roles, purposes, accountabilities, domains, policies) through proposals. The Secretary will update the governance records from these minutes, so the exact wording of proposals, integrations and adopted texts matters more than brevity.

Summarize the discussion, but leave nothing out: every agenda item, proposal, clarifying question, reaction, objection, integration and election gets its bullet, even a short one. Never summarize the text of a proposal, an amendment, an integration or an adopted change: report it as close to verbatim as possible.

How to write it:

- Only the sections below, in this order, each with its `###` heading: no title, no other sections (no attendance, no check-in, no closing).
- Bullets, short sentences, no preambles. Sub-bullets for details.
- Write people by their name only, never with the "(Speaker N)" label the Transcript puts after it, and never as "Me". Add their role in brackets where the Transcript says it, e.g. "Mario Rossi (Secretary)".
- Leave out the personal content of the check-in and closing rounds, greetings and problems with the call.
- Recognition gets words wrong: write the correct term when the context makes it certain (Holacracy terms such as "tension", "accountability", "domain", "policy", "Rep Link", in Italian "cerchia" for circle, never "circolo"; tools such as "Holaspirit" or "GlassFrog"; role names as the personal notes or the invited participants write them). A word or a passage of a proposal not clear from the Transcript gets "[TO VERIFY]" right after it.
- Never infer the outcome of a proposal or the result of an objection test: write only what is stated.
- A section with nothing to put in it is left out, heading included. Never a line saying something is missing or not stated.

### Administrative Concerns
The logistics raised (timing, absences, schedule), one bullet each.

### Agenda
One bullet per agenda item, in the order it was raised: `Label — who raised it`.

### Proposal: Label
One such section for each agenda item, in the order it was processed, with the item's label and these parts in bold, each one only if it took place:
- `**Proposer:** Name · **Tension:** the tension in one sentence`.
- `**Proposal**`: the text of the initial proposal as a quote (`>`), organized by type of change: new role, change to a purpose, accountability or domain, role removal, policy.
- `**Clarifying questions**`: each question and its answer, one bullet each.
- `**Reactions**`: each reaction in one bullet, with who gave it.
- `**Amend and clarify**`: what the proposer changed or explained after the reactions, the changed text as a quote.
- `**Objections**`: one bullet per objection: who raised it, its content in one sentence, and the test result, **valid** or **not valid**, with the reason in a sub-bullet.
- `**Integration**`: for each valid objection, how the proposal was changed to integrate it, and the changed text as a quote.
- `**Outcome:** Adopted`, Withdrawn or Not concluded, as stated.
- `**Adopted text**`: when adopted, the final text complete as a quote, ready to copy into the governance records.

### Governance Changes
Every change adopted in the meeting, grouped by role or policy (e.g. "Role X — accountability added: …"), one bullet each: the checklist the Secretary will use to update the governance records.

### Elections
For each election (Facilitator, Secretary, Rep Link…): the role, the person elected and the term if stated.

### Unprocessed Items
The agenda items still open at the end of the meeting, one bullet each, to carry over to the next Governance Meeting.

Write every heading, these ones included, in the language of the summary; keep the role names as they are.
